R3U|Phone Link

Use your Android phone from your computer: photos, notifications, messages, files. Over your own Wi-Fi. No cable, no cloud, no account.

  1. 1

    Get the app

    Download the Android app to your phone.

    Download APK

    Your browser will ask to allow Install unknown apps for it. Approve that once. Google Play Protect may also show a warning because the app isn’t from the Play Store; choose Install anyway.

  2. 2

    Start the app

    Open R3U | Phone Link on your phone, allow access to all photos, then confirm with your fingerprint, face, or passcode. The server starts by itself.

    Your phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. 3

    Open Phone Link

    On your computer, open the link shown in the app (or scan its QR code with your computer’s camera app).

This page is only a doorway. Your photos never touch this site. No tracking, no cookies.
